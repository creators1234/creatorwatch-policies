CreatorWatch — Terms of Service

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Operator and contact

The service operator is CreatorWatch. Contact: creatorwatch3@gmail.com.

What the service does

CreatorWatch helps participating creators identify links that may contain unauthorised copies of their content. It checks configured websites using creator aliases, records discovered page links and status changes, and sends updates through Discord and email. It can provide draft copyright removal notices for creators to review and send themselves.

CreatorWatch does not download or store images or videos. A discovered link is a potential match, not a determination of ownership or infringement. A link marked removed means our checks indicate that the monitored page is unavailable; it does not prove that all copies have been deleted.

Who may use it

Use the service only for your own content or where you have permission to act for the relevant creator or rights holder. Provide accurate aliases, contact details and ownership information. Creators must authorise monitoring before being added. Access is arranged by the operator; the administration website is private.

Responsible use

Do not use CreatorWatch to harass people, investigate someone without authority, distribute intimate material, access another creator's reports, or submit false or misleading copyright claims. Follow applicable law and Discord's terms and community rules. Keep private channel information confidential and report incorrect matches or unintended access to the operator.

Notices and decisions

You must check each link and the accuracy, legal basis and recipient of any removal notice before sending it. CreatorWatch sends email drafts to the participating creator, not automatically to the website's copyright contact. Templates are administrative assistance, not legal advice or legal representation. Providing an alias or requesting monitoring does not transfer ownership of your work.

Availability and limits

Coverage depends on configured sites, their availability, access restrictions and page structure. Matches may be incomplete or incorrect, and notifications may be delayed. We do not guarantee discovery of every copy, uninterrupted operation, a successful takedown, or any particular response from a third party.

Nothing in these terms excludes rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded, including applicable statutory consumer rights.

Privacy and third parties

The Privacy Policy explains how information is handled. Discord and email providers process information delivered through their services under their own terms and policies. Discord server administrators may be able to access private channels. Opening a reported link connects you to a third-party website, whose content and practices we do not control.

Stopping or changing the service

You can ask the operator to stop monitoring or to delete your information using the contact above. Stopping scans and deleting stored records are separate actions, as explained in the Privacy Policy. The operator may suspend access to address misuse, security concerns or operational problems.

We may update these terms as the service changes. The updated date will appear here, and material changes affecting participating creators will be communicated through their existing contact channel before they take effect where practicable. Contact the operator if you do not wish to continue.
