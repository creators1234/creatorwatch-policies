CreatorWatch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Who is responsible

The service operator is CreatorWatch, who is responsible for its processing of personal information. For privacy questions, correction requests, requests to stop monitoring or deletion requests, contact creatorwatch3@gmail.com. Please contact us privately rather than putting creator details or discovered links in public GitHub issues.

Information we handle

The operator enters information provided by creators or their authorised representatives: creator/display names, aliases, email addresses, Discord user IDs and, where provided, legal names and original content URLs. We also handle server and channel IDs needed to deliver updates.

Configured public websites supply page URLs and matching text or metadata. For regular monitoring, we store discovered links, associated creator and site details, discovery/check times, availability status, scan outcomes, errors and notification records. Notification records may contain a copy of the message or email draft and its delivery status. Requests sent to websites can include the alias in the URL.

CreatorWatch reads HTML and link metadata. It does not download or store images or videos. It does not continuously read Discord conversations. Messages you voluntarily send to the operator are separately handled through the communication service you use.

Ad hoc scan results are displayed temporarily and are not saved in the monitoring database or added to notification queues. Websites and network/service providers may still process requests and technical logs.

Why we use information

We use information to find possible unauthorised copies, avoid duplicate reports, check whether pages remain available, provide creator reports, deliver private Discord updates and email drafts, maintain the service and respond to support or privacy requests. Automated matches assist human review; they do not decide infringement or make legal decisions about a person.

Authorisation and legal basis

Creators authorise monitoring before they are added. We rely on their consent to monitor their supplied aliases and send the requested updates. They can withdraw that consent by contacting us; withdrawal stops future consent-based processing and does not make earlier processing unlawful. Providing information is voluntary, but we need the relevant aliases and delivery details to provide monitoring and notifications.

Links and aliases can reveal sensitive information about a creator, including information about their sex life. Where this applies, we require the creator's explicit consent to that processing before monitoring begins. General agreement to these terms alone is not a substitute for that explicit consent.

We rely on our legitimate interests in securing and administering the service for necessary security and operational records, and on legal obligations where we are required to retain or disclose information. We use only information needed for those purposes and consider the interests and rights of the people affected.

Who receives it

  • The operator can access monitoring records and administer the service.
  • Discord processes channel identifiers and the updates posted for a creator. Channels are configured for the creator, operator and bot; server owners and administrators can also have access under Discord's permissions model.
  • Google/Gmail and the recipient's email provider process email addresses, message contents and delivery information. Draft notices are sent to the creator. If the creator forwards a notice, its recipients receive the information included in it.
  • Monitored websites, and the proxy/VPN and network services used to reach them, process requests necessary to perform checks. A VPN does not make the alias in a requested URL invisible to the destination website.
  • GitHub hosts these public policy pages and may process visitor IP addresses and request information. Creator records and the private application database are not published here.

We do not sell creator information or use it for advertising. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or as necessary to address a security incident or legal claim. Third-party providers may process information outside your country; their own privacy notices explain their processing and applicable transfer arrangements.

Storage, retention and deletion

The administration website and database are hosted on the operator's private server. Access is restricted, and integration credentials are encrypted in the application. These measures do not imply that every database record or every third-party copy is encrypted end to end.

We retain records while needed for authorised monitoring, related support or a specific legal obligation or claim. Deletion is managed by the operator: regular monitoring records remain until the operator removes them; there is currently no automatic age-based expiry for creator, finding or delivery records. Disabling a creator or website stops the relevant future monitoring but does not erase existing records. Local database backups retain the most recent 14 daily backup files, so older copies rotate out as new backups are made.

You can request deletion or cessation of monitoring using the contact above. We may need reasonable information to verify your authority. We will explain any information that must be retained to meet a legal obligation or handle a legal claim. Deletion from the local application does not automatically remove messages already posted to Discord or copies delivered by email; those copies must be handled separately through the relevant accounts and providers.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to its processing, and receive certain information in a portable form. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact us to exercise these rights or raise a concern. You can also complain to your data protection regulator; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

Cookies and policy updates

These policy pages contain no application login or advertising trackers added by CreatorWatch. The private administration website uses session and security cookies. Hosting and communication providers have their own policies, including https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement, https://discord.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We will update the date above when this policy changes and communicate material changes to participating creators through their existing contact channel.
